Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between contested and uncontested divorce?+

Uncontested means you and your spouse agree on the terms — it's faster and cheaper. Contested means you don't, and the court decides. We handle both and try to resolve contested cases efficiently where possible.

How long does a divorce take?+

Uncontested divorces can move quickly. Contested cases take longer and depend on the issues. We give you a realistic timeline after reviewing your situation.

Will I have to go to court?+

For uncontested cases, often not. For contested cases, we attend the hearings for you and prepare you for any where your presence matters.

Can you help with custody and support too?+

Yes — custody, visitation, and child support are central parts of most divorces, and we fight for arrangements that protect your children and your rights.
