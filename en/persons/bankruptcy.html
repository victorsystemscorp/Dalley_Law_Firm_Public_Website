Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose everything if I file bankruptcy?+

No. Most Chapter 7 filers keep their home, car, and belongings thanks to exemptions. We review your assets and protect everything the law allows.

How fast do the collections calls stop?+

The moment your case is filed, an automatic stay goes into effect. Collectors, garnishments, and most lawsuits must stop immediately.

How much does it cost to start?+

We can begin your case with an initial payment as low as $300, with a fixed-fee plan you can budget for. We give you the full estimate up front.

Will bankruptcy ruin my credit forever?+

No. Bankruptcy stays on your report for a limited time, and many clients rebuild credit quickly afterward. We guide you on repairing credit after discharge.
