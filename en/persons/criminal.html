Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I talk to the police without a lawyer?+

No. Anything you say can be used against you — and against your immigration case. Exercise your right to remain silent and call us first.

Can a small offense really affect my immigration status?+

Yes. Even minor offenses can make you inadmissible or deportable, or block naturalization. We look for deals that avoid or reduce those consequences.

Will you handle both my criminal and immigration cases?+

Yes — that coordinated approach is exactly what protects you. We analyze both systems together so one resolution doesn't quietly sabotage the other.

How quickly can you get involved?+

Immediately. Call us as soon as you are contacted by police or arrested. Early involvement often means better deals, faster release, and fewer lasting consequences.
