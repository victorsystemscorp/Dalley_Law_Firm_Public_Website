Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I was served with a lawsuit &mdash; now what?+

Don't ignore it. You usually have a short window to respond or you risk a default judgment. Call us immediately and we'll prepare and file your answer in time.

Someone owes me money &mdash; can you help?+

Yes. Often a strong demand letter resolves it. If not, we file the claim and pursue collection aggressively, including small claims when that fits.

Will my case have to go to trial?+

Most disputes settle. We negotiate hard for a fair deal, but we prepare every case as if it will go to trial — so you're protected either way.

Can I get a fixed fee?+

Many dispute matters qualify for a fixed-fee estimate. After a quick review of your case we tell you up front what it will cost.
