Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I received a tax notice &mdash; what should I do?+

Don't ignore it, and don't respond alone. Notices often have tight deadlines and saying the wrong thing can cost you. We review the notice and respond correctly.

I&apos;m being audited. Do I need a lawyer?+

An audit can turn into a much bigger problem if mishandled. We coordinate with your accountant, manage communications, and protect your rights throughout the process.

My personal and business accounts are mixed &mdash; is that bad?+

Yes — it can pierce your liability protection and complicate taxes. We help you separate accounts properly and unwind the problem before it grows.

Do you replace my accountant?+

No — we work alongside your accountant as legal counsel, especially where compliance, disputes, or government action is involved. You get both perspectives working for you.
