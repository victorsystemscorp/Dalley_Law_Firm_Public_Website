The Problem

Leases Are Written to Protect the Landlord — Not You

A commercial lease can lock you into rent increases, hidden costs, personal guarantees, and renewal traps that quietly drain your business for years. Most owners sign what's put in front of them, only to learn the problems after they're bound.

A review before you sign is cheap insurance against expensive surprises.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

I already have a lease &mdash; is it too late?+

Not always. Some terms can be re-negotiated at renewal or amendment. Even if you're bound, a review tells you exactly what you're exposed to so you can plan ahead.

Landlord says their lease is &quot;standard.&quot; True?+

There is no truly standard lease — clauses are heavily skewed toward the landlord. We translate what each clause really means for your business and flag the dangerous ones.

What does a lease review cost?+

Lease review is typically a fixed-fee service based on length and complexity. You know the price before we start, so you can decide with confidence.

Can you negotiate for me?+

Yes. After the review we can negotiate directly with the landlord's attorney or broker to change the terms that put you at risk.
