Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get the best price for my business?+

Price is shaped by structure, terms, and how liabilities are allocated. We negotiate the deal so the number that matters — what you keep — is as strong as possible.

Can I still be sued after I sell?+

Possibly — depending on structure and warranties. We draft the agreement to limit post-sale claims and, where possible, structure around them so you truly move on.

What about my lease?+

Most leases need landlord approval to assign. We handle the assignment and negotiate with the landlord so it doesn't block your sale or trap you personally.

What does selling cost?+

We give you a fixed-fee estimate based on the deal's size and complexity, so your legal costs are clear from the start.
