Hidden Debts and Bad Terms You Don't See Coming

Behind any business for sale there can be unpaid taxes, lawsuits, lease obligations, employee claims, or contracts that transfer to the new owner. Buyers who skip legal review often inherit these problems along with the business.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need a lawyer to buy a small business?+

Yes. The price may seem fair, but the liabilities hiding inside the business can cost you far more. Legal review protects what you're actually buying.

Asset purchase or stock purchase &mdash; what&apos;s the difference?+

In an asset purchase you buy selected assets and usually avoid old liabilities; in a stock purchase you buy the company itself, often including its debts. We recommend the structure that protects you.

Can you help negotiate the price?+

Our job is to make the deal terms safe and fair. We negotiate protections, price adjustments, and seller obligations that reflect what due diligence reveals.

How much does this cost?+

We give you a fixed-fee estimate after learning the deal's size and complexity, so you know your legal costs before you commit.
