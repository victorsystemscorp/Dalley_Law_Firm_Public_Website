Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to trademark my business name?+

If your name matters to your brand, yes — a trademark is how you prevent others from using it and how you stop copycats. We assess and file where it helps.

My content was copied &mdash; what can I do?+

Often a well-crafted cease-and-desist resolves it. If not, we pursue further enforcement. Acting quickly usually improves outcomes.

Someone claims I infringed on them &mdash; now what?+

Don't respond on your own. We review the claim, assess its strength, and respond in a way that protects you without making things worse.

Can you register my trademark or copyright?+

Yes. We handle filing and prosecution for many marks and works, giving you enforceable rights and clear next steps.
