Who We Are

Our Firm & Attorneys

We have attorneys with experience in multiple areas of the law. Our mission is to provide excellent service through highly experienced attorneys, always protecting our clients' interests.

The firm specializes in serving the immigrant community. We have been serving the community for the last 25 years and will continue to do so in the future. Our services range from quick and easy assistance to a full range of legal services — from criminal defense to immigration, and much more. Immigrants in New York City need a trusted law firm that understands the immigration consequences of different legal situations, and we are ready to serve. The firm has a proven track record and is committed to helping the immigrant community in every way possible.
